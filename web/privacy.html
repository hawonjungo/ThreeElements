Injoker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Injoker is a small game made by an independent developer (contact below). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Injoker on Android (the app on Google Play) or in a web browser (injoker.relifes.net).

The short version

  • Injoker does not ask for an account, and does not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking in the game.
  • Your progress stays on your own device.

Android app

The Android app has no internet permission: it cannot send anything anywhere. It stores a few things on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • your records (best score, best combo, best survival time) and your 10 best runs,
  • whether sound is on, and whether you have finished the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Web version

In a browser the same data is kept in the browser's local storage on your device. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

The web page also shows a visitor counter. The first time a browser opens the page, it sends one anonymous request to a free counting service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) to add one visit; no name, account or identifier from the game is sent. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services receive the technical details of each request (such as your IP address) as part of normal internet traffic; Injoker does not collect or keep them. See the GitHub privacy statement for GitHub Pages.

Children

Injoker is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If Injoker ever adds features that handle personal data (for example online leaderboards with Google Play Games sign-in), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.
