How to play

Three keys. Ten spells.

Every enemy needs one spell. Its name is on screen; the recipe is up to you.

QWE

Load elements

Quas (ice), Wex (lightning) and Exort (fire). Up to three orbs; a fourth pushes out the oldest.

R

Invoke

Your three orbs become a spell. It goes into slot D, and your previous spell moves to F.

DF

Cast

Hit the enemy with the spell it needs. Right spell: it is gone and your combo grows.

Order does not matter, only the mix: QQW = QWQ = WQQ. On a phone, the same buttons are on screen.

Game modes

Every run gets faster

MAIN MODE

Play

Normal enemies need one spell. Elites need a chain of two, overlords a chain of three, in order. Break a chain and every spell counts: an enemy that reaches you costs one life for each spell left.

  • Beat an overlord for a rune: extra life, frost, double score, bounty or shield
  • Earn gold, move on to the next stage
  • Climb the world ranking by score
BOSS FIGHTS

Immortals

Five great foes that only fall to a real combo of 4 to 8 spells. Lift them with Tornado, then land EMP, Chaos Meteor, Sun Strike and Deafening Blast as they come down.

  • PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD: timing decides the damage
  • They appear by chance in PLAY; practise each one from the menu
  • Only Immortals drop the materials for item upgrades
SHOP

Items & runes

Spend the gold you earn on items for your six slots: Blink Dagger, Eul's Scepter, Black King Bar, Hand of Midas, Refresher Orb and more, each with an upgrade.

  • Gold only comes from playing: no purchases, ever
  • Upgrades need materials from Immortals
ENDLESS

Survival & tutorial

Survival is the pure test: one spell per enemy, three hearts, rising speed. How long can you last? New to it? A short tutorial walks you through your first spell, key by key.

  • Score, combo, accuracy and survival time
  • Ranked worldwide by time

FAQ

Questions

Is Injoker free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. The gold for the shop only comes from playing.

What do I need to play?

A modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer with a keyboard or on a phone or tablet with the on-screen buttons. Turn the phone sideways for the best view.

When is the Android app coming?

Soon on Google Play. Follow us on Facebook to hear the day it is out. Until then the web version has the same game.

Can I play on iPhone or iPad?

Yes, in Safari. For full screen, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Injoker from its icon.

Where are my records kept?

On your device, in the browser. The world ranking is optional: you choose a name the first time you open it.

Is this an official Dota 2 game?

No. Injoker is an independent fan-made practice game, inspired by a famous spell-mixing hero. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation.
