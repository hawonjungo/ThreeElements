Injoker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Injoker is a small game made by an independent developer ("we", contact below). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Injoker on Android (the app net.relifes.injoker on Google Play) or in a web browser (injoker.relifes.net).

The short version

  • Injoker does not need an account, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking in the game.
  • Your progress stays on your own device.
  • One optional feature of the Android app uses Google: the global leaderboards. Only if you sign in to Google Play Games are your results sent to Google, to rank you. See below.

Android app

The Android app requests no permissions. It stores a few things on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • your records (best score, best combo, best survival time), your 10 best runs in each mode, your best time against each boss, your gold, the materials you found and the items you bought in the shop (with game gold only),
  • your settings: sound on/off, recipe hint on/off, and whether you have finished the tutorial.

We cannot see this data. Unless you use the global leaderboards described next, it never leaves your device.

Global leaderboards (Android, optional)

From version 1.7.0 the Android app can show global leaderboards through Google Play Games. This is optional: the whole game works without signing in, and a player who is not signed in is not ranked globally and sends nothing.

  • If you have a Google Play Games profile, Google may sign you in to it when the game starts; otherwise you are only asked when you open the global ranking yourself.
  • While you are signed in, the game sends two kinds of results to Google Play Games: your score in PLAY and your survival time in SURVIVAL. They are stored by Google with your Play Games profile and shown on the leaderboards next to your Play Games name and avatar, to the people your Play Games settings allow.
  • The game itself never reads, stores or sends your name, avatar, e-mail address or anything else from your Google account. We run no server of our own and receive none of this.
  • Google processes this information under its own privacy policy.

Web version

In a browser the same data is kept in the browser's local storage on your device.

The web page also shows a visitor counter. The first time a browser opens the page, it sends one anonymous request to a free counting service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) to add one visit; no name, account or identifier from the game is sent. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services receive the technical details of each request (such as your IP address) as part of normal internet traffic; Injoker does not collect or keep them. See the GitHub privacy statement for GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

What the game stores is on your device, so you are always in control of it:

  • Android: uninstall the app, or open Settings → Apps → Injoker → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Web: clear the site data for injoker.relifes.net in your browser settings.
  • Global leaderboards: your results there belong to your Google Play Games profile. In the Play Games app, open Settings to delete your data for a single game or your whole Play Games profile; you can also hide your game activity there. To stop sending results, sign out of Play Games for Injoker in the same place.

We hold no copy of your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Google Play

If you install Injoker from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and device under its own privacy policy. We only receive the aggregated, anonymous statistics Google Play Console shows every developer (such as install counts and crash reports).

Children

Injoker is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
